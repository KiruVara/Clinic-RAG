# Parking and Accessibility

Our clinic is committed to providing accessible services to all patients.

## Parking

- Free parking is available in the clinic lot.
- Two accessible parking spaces are located near the main entrance.
- Overflow parking is available on Maple Grove Road.

## Accessibility Features

- Automatic doors at the main entrance.
- Wheelchair-accessible washrooms.
- Adjustable-height exam tables in select rooms.

## Transportation

- Durham Region Transit Route 902 stops directly in front of the clinic.
- Patients using mobility services may request curbside assistance by calling reception.

## Winter Safety

During winter months, snow clearing begins at 6:00 AM. Salt is applied regularly, but patients should use caution on icy days.
