# Patient Privacy Policy (PHIPA-Compliant)

Our clinic follows the Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) to protect patient privacy and confidentiality.

## Collection of Personal Health Information

We collect personal health information such as:
- Medical history
- Contact information
- OHIP details
- Diagnostic results
- Referral documents

This information is used to provide safe and effective healthcare.

## Access to Records

- Only authorized clinical staff may access patient records.
- Patients may request a copy of their records with valid identification.
- Requests are processed within a reasonable timeframe as required by PHIPA.

## Consent and Disclosure

- Patient consent is required before sharing information with third parties, except when required by law.
- Information may be shared with other healthcare providers involved in the patient’s care.
- De-identified data may be used for quality improvement.

## Storage and Security

- Records are stored in a secure electronic medical record (EMR) system.
- Backups are encrypted and stored according to PHIPA guidelines.
