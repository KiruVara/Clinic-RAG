# Privacy and Records Requests 

Our clinic follows PHIPA guidelines for managing personal health information.

## Requesting Records

- Patients may request records by submitting a written form.
- Processing time is typically 7–10 business days.

## Fees

- OHIP does not cover record printing.
- Fees follow OMA uninsured service guidelines.

## Third-Party Requests

- Legal, insurance, and employer requests require signed consent.
