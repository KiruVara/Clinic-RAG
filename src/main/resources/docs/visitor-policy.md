# Visitor Policy

Maple Grove Community Clinic welcomes visitors who accompany patients to their appointments. To maintain safety and privacy, all visitors must follow the guidelines below.

## General Rules

- Visitors must check in at reception upon arrival.
- Only one visitor may accompany a patient into the exam room unless special circumstances apply.
- Children under 12 must be supervised at all times.

## Infection Control

- Visitors with symptoms such as fever, cough, or vomiting should not enter the clinic.
- Masks may be required during respiratory illness seasons, following Ontario Public Health guidance.

## Privacy Considerations

- Visitors may be asked to step out during sensitive discussions.
- Recording devices are not permitted inside exam rooms.

## Visiting Hours

Visitors are welcome during regular clinic hours:
- Monday–Friday: 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
