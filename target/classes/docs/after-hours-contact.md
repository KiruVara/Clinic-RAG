# After-Hours Contact

Our clinic provides limited after-hours support for urgent medical concerns.

## When to Use After-Hours Service

- Worsening symptoms that cannot wait until morning.
- Medication side effects.
- Urgent follow-up questions after a recent appointment.

## Contact Options

- After-hours line: 905-555-2022 (available until 8:00 PM)
- Telehealth Ontario: 1-866-797-0000 (24/7)
- For emergencies, call 911 or visit the nearest hospital.

## Limitations

- No prescription renewals after hours.
- No form completion or administrative requests.
- Overnight stays are not permitted. The clinic does not operate 24-hour inpatient services.
