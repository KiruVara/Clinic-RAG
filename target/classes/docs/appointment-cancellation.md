# Appointment Cancellation Policy

To ensure timely access to care, Maple Grove Community Clinic enforces a clear cancellation policy.

## Cancellation Requirements

- Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.
- Same-day cancellations may be recorded in the patient chart.

## No-Show Fees

- A no-show fee of $40 may apply for missed appointments.
- Fees follow Ontario Medical Association (OMA) uninsured service guidelines.

## Repeated Missed Appointments

- After two no-shows within six months, patients may be required to book through reception only.
- Chronic no-shows may result in limited appointment availability.

## How to Cancel

Patients may cancel by:
- Phone: 905-555-1022
- Online portal
- Emailing reception@maplegroveclinic.ca
