# Appointment Booking Policy

Our clinic provides primary care services to patients in Ontario. Appointments can be booked online, by phone, or in person.

## Health Card Requirements

- Patients must present a valid Ontario health card at each visit.
- Health card numbers are verified through the Ministry of Health system.
- If a health card is expired or invalid, the visit may be billed privately.

## Booking Rules

- Appointments are typically booked in 15–30 minute intervals depending on the reason for visit.
- New patients must complete a registration form and provide medical history.
- Referrals from other healthcare providers must include relevant documentation.

## Walk-In Availability

- Walk-in visits are offered based on daily capacity.
- Walk-in patients are triaged and seen in order of medical urgency.

## Cancellation Policy

- Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment.
- Same-day cancellations may be recorded in the patient file.

## No-Show Policy

- A no-show occurs when a patient does not attend their appointment without notice.
- After two no-shows, the clinic may apply a no-show fee or restrict future bookings.
