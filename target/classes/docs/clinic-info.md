# Maple Grove Community Clinic — General Information

Maple Grove Community Clinic is a family medicine and primary care clinic serving patients in Ontario. We provide appointment-based and limited walk-in services, with a focus on accessible, community-centered care.

The clinic is located near public transit routes and offers free on-site parking for patients and visitors.

## Hours of Operation

- **Monday–Friday:** 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  The clinic is not open on Sundays. We do not provide any services on Sunday.
  Sunday hours: closed. The clinic does not operate on Sundays.


The clinic does not provide 24-hour or inpatient services. Patients and/or visitors are not allowed overnight.

## Services Offered

- Family medicine and primary care
- Prescription renewals
- Routine blood work
- Vaccinations
- Chronic disease management
- Referrals to specialists
- Telehealth appointments

## Additional Notes

- Patients must present a valid Ontario health card at each visit.
- The clinic responds to non-urgent inquiries within 2–3 business days.
- For medical emergencies, patients should call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department.
