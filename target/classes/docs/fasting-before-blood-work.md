# Fasting Before Blood Work

Some blood tests require fasting to ensure accurate results.

## Fasting Requirements

- Fasting typically means no food for 8–12 hours.
- Water is allowed and encouraged.
- Black coffee or tea may be permitted unless otherwise instructed.

## Tests That Require Fasting

- Lipid panel
- Fasting glucose
- Certain metabolic tests

## Morning Appointments

Patients who struggle with fasting may book early morning appointments between 8:30 AM and 10:00 AM.
