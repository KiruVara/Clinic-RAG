# Medication Refill Process – Maple Grove Community Clinic

Our clinic provides timely medication refills while ensuring safe prescribing practices.

## Refill Timeline

- Please request refills 7 days before running out.
- Urgent same-day refill requests may not be guaranteed.

## How to Request

- Through the online patient portal.
- By calling reception.
- Through your pharmacy’s fax request.

## Controlled Substances

- Narcotics, stimulants, and benzodiazepines require an appointment.
- Early refill requests for controlled medications are not permitted.

## OHIP Coverage

Medication reviews and renewals are covered under OHIP when medically necessary.
