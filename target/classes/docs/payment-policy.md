# Payment and Billing Policy

Our clinic follows Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP) billing rules and offers private payment options when services are not covered.

## OHIP Coverage

- Most medically necessary services are covered by OHIP.
- Patients must present a valid health card at each visit.
- Services not covered by OHIP may require private payment.

## Uninsured Services

Examples of uninsured services include:
- Sick notes for work or school
- Driver’s medical forms
- Travel medicine consultations
- Missed appointment fees

Fees for uninsured services follow the Ontario Medical Association (OMA) guidelines.

## Private Payments

- Private payments can be made by debit, credit, or e-transfer.
- Receipts are provided for all private transactions.

## Outstanding Balances

- Patients with outstanding balances will receive an invoice.
- Accounts overdue by more than 60 days may require payment before booking future appointments.
