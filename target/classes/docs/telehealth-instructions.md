# Telehealth Instructions

Telehealth appointments allow patients to receive care remotely.

## Before the Appointment

- Ensure your phone or computer is charged.
- Have your health card ready.
- Join the video link 5 minutes early.

## During the Appointment

- Choose a quiet, private space.
- The physician may request photos of rashes or injuries.

## Limitations

- Telehealth cannot be used for controlled substance prescriptions.
- Some assessments require in-person follow-up.
